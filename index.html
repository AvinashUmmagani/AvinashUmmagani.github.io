Who I Am

I'm an Android Engineer based in Hyderabad, India, specializing in AOSP-based system application development for embedded STB and DTH consumer platforms.

At Caavo Labs, I've worked on Falcon — an Android 11 AOSP DTH platform — migrating legacy monolithic apps into scalable modular systems, building hardware-integrated features using HDMI-CEC and BLE, and shipping user-facing experiences from Setup Wizards to video calling.

I care deeply about clean architecture, well-structured codebases, and the intersection of software and hardware on embedded Android platforms.
